# OpenET training resources

### [Farm and Ranch Management Support (FARMS)](https://farms.etdata.org/)

The FARMS user interface was developed to deliver tailored reports of OpenET data for specified regions and locations of interest. FARMS allows users to load custom boundaries, select from existing boundaries, or draw their own boundary. Users can then create custom reports by selecting the variables (e.g. actual ET, the fraction of ET relative to reference ET, or reference ET), assign a time period and define the frequency of ET data (e.g. daily monthly). Compared to the Data Explorer, FARMS saves user-defined boundaries of interest and supports automated updated reports to reflect the most recent OpenET data. The FARMS documentation can be found here.

### [ET Irrigation Water Balance Dashboard](https://et-irrigate.com)

The ET Irrigation Water Balance Dashboard was developed to directly integrate OpenET data into an irrigation scheduling decision-support tool. Inspired by the wET graph developed by NRCS collaborators, the dashboard enables users to evaluate soil water storage and make informed decisions about the timing and amount of irrigation applications. Automated workflows retrieve OpenET evapotranspiration (ET) data and precipitation estimates for a field of interest, while users can enter irrigation events to automatically update the root-zone water balance. Soil properties can either be entered manually or retrieved automatically from NRCS data sources. The tool is under active development, and we welcome feedback to help further improve its functionality and usefulness.
